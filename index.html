OUR HISTORY

FC Vezhdanisht was officially founded in 2023 by Aurel Zyka, with the vital support of team legend Luan Zyka. Together, their vision was to build a club grounded in discipline, unity, and a relentless competitive drive.

What began as a passion project quickly took shape into a fully organized squad. Under their leadership, the club established its core identity, bringing together dedicated players focused on making an immediate impact on the pitch.

The early years laid a solid foundation in local competition:

  • 2024 FC Vezhdanisht competed in the Rupel League, finishing a respectable 5th place in their campaign.
  • 2025 Building on their experience, the team delivered another consistent season in the Rupel Liga, securing a 6th place finish.
  • 2026 The major breakthrough came in 2026, when FC Vezhdanisht officially stepped up to Provincial 2 Antwerp (RBFA). Backed by key new transfers and a re-energized roster, the club built a formidable team that hit the ground running with a strong start to the season.

Today, FC Vezhdanisht continues to push its boundaries—driven by the vision of its founder, the guidance of its club legend, and the ambition of every player wearing the badge.
